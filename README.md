# **THE NOTEBOOK (2004)**

## A. Introduction

**The Notebook** is a romantic drama movie released in **2004** and directed by Nick Cassavetes. The movie tells the love story of Noah Calhoun and Allie Hamilton, two people from different social backgrounds. It shows how love can survive challenges, separation, and the passage of time.

## B. Synopsis
The story follows Noah Calhoun and Allie Hamilton, who meet during the summer of 1940 in South Carolina. Even though they come from different social classes, they fall in love and spend a memorable summer together. However, Allie's parents do not approve of their relationship, which causes them to separate. Noah writes letters to Allie, but she never receives them because her mother hides them. Years later, Allie becomes engaged to another man named Lon Hammond, while Noah continues to remember their love. When Allie sees a newspaper article about Noah restoring an old house, she decides to visit him. Their reunion brings back their feelings, and Allie must decide what she truly wants in life.

> True love is about choosing someone even when life becomes difficult.

## C. Characters

1. Noah Calhoun - A hardworking and caring young man who deeply loves Allie.
2. Allie Hamilton - A young woman from a wealthy family who follows her heart despite the challenges.
3. Lon Hammond Jr. - Allie's kind and successful fiancé who wants to marry her.
4. Anne Hamilton - Allie's mother, who initially disapproves of Noah because of his social status.
5. Duke - An elderly man who reads a love story to a woman in a nursing home.

## D. Personal Review

I chose The Notebook because I love romantic movies, especially stories that show how deep and meaningful love can be. This movie made me realize that love is not always easy, but it requires patience, understanding, and commitment. I also liked how the story showed the importance of memories and the people we love.

1. The Love Story - I liked how Noah and Allie continued to love each other despite the challenges they faced.
2. The Characters - I enjoyed the chemistry between Noah and Allie because their emotions felt real and natural.
3. The Emotional Scenes - Some scenes were heartbreaking, but they made the story more meaningful and unforgettable.

### My Personal Rating
